\section{Proposed Solutions}

\subsection{Overall Solution}

The proposed solution is an AI-powered digital platform that connects film photography enthusiasts with Film Processing Labs through a centralized ecosystem.

The platform provides a unified environment where photographers can discover Film Labs, compare services, make bookings, track processing orders, manage digital film archives, and interact with the film photography community.

For Film Lab owners, the system provides management tools for handling services, pricing, orders, customers, and operational workflows.

\subsection{System Roles}

The platform supports the following main user roles:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Photographer:} Users who search for Film Labs, book services, track orders, manage digital archives, and interact with the community.
    
    \item \textbf{Film Lab Owner:} Service providers who manage Film Lab profiles, services, pricing, customer orders, and processing workflows.
    
    \item \textbf{Delivery Partner:} Users or service providers responsible for pickup and delivery requests.
    
    \item \textbf{System Administrator:} Users responsible for managing the overall platform, users, services, and system operations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{System Features}

The proposed platform integrates several key features:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Film Lab discovery and comparison.
    \item Service booking and order management.
    \item Pickup and delivery request management.
    \item Real-time order tracking.
    \item Digital film archive management.
    \item Marketplace and community interaction.
    \item AI-powered photography assistance and recommendations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Expected Benefits}

The proposed solution is expected to provide benefits for both photographers and Film Labs.

For photographers, the platform simplifies the process of finding reliable Film Labs, comparing services, making bookings, and tracking film processing orders.

For Film Labs, the platform provides a centralized management system that improves service visibility, customer communication, and operational efficiency.

For the film photography community, the platform creates a digital ecosystem that supports knowledge sharing, community interaction, and the long-term preservation of film photography.
