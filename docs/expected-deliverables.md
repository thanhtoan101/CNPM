# Expected Deliverables
The expected deliverables of the AI-powered Film Photography Playform include the following products:
## 1. Mobile Application for Photographers
A Flutter mobile application for photographers to search for Film Labs, book film developing, scanning, and sprinting services, track order progress, receive digital scans, manage their Digital Film Archive, use the Marketplace, and interact with the AI Assistant.
## 2. Film Lab Management Portal
A web-based management portal that allows Film Lab owners to manage laboratory profiles, service packages, pricing, customer orders, processing workflows, digital scan delivery, revenue, customers, and operational reports.
## 3. Web Administration System
A web administration system for managing users, Film Labs, permissions, service categories, marketplace activities, payments, reviews, complaints, disputes, and overall platform performance.
## 4. Marketplace Module
A marketplace module that allows community members to buy, sell, or exchange analog cameras, lenses, film rolls, and photography accesories.
## 5. Community and Knowledge-Sharing Platform
A community platform where photography experts and users can publish tutorials, technical articles, qeuiment reviews, Film Lab reviews, workshops, photowalk events, and photography experiences.
## 6. Digital Film Archive Management System
A digital asset management system that allows photoraphers to view, dowload, organize, and permanently archive scanned photographs and related metadata.
## 7. AI Recommendation Engine
An AI-based recommendation system that suggests suitable Film Lab, film tupes, services, and educational resources based on user preferences, location, service history, community feedback.
## 8. AI-powered Photography Assistant
A domain-specific AI Asisstant using Large Language Models and Retrieval-Augmented Generation to answer questions related to analog photography.
## 9. Backend Services and Cloud Infrastructure
Backend APIs, database systems, authentication services, cloud storage, search services, payment and logistics integrations, backup mechanisms, deployment pipelines, and cloud infrastructure required to operate the platform.
