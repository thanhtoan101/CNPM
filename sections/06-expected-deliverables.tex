\section{Expected Deliverables}

The project is expected to deliver a complete digital ecosystem that supports the major activities of the analog photography community. The final system will include multiple integrated products for photographers, Film Lab owners, administrators, and community members.

\subsection{Mobile Application for Photographers}

A cross-platform mobile application will be developed for photographers using Flutter. The application will support major user activities, including Film Lab discovery, service comparison, service booking, order tracking, digital scan management, marketplace interaction, and communication with the AI Assistant.

Key functions of the mobile application include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item User registration and account management.
    \item Intelligent Film Lab discovery and comparison.
    \item Film developing, scanning, and printing service booking.
    \item Pickup and delivery request management.
    \item Real-time order tracking.
    \item Online payment integration.
    \item Digital scan viewing and downloading.
    \item Personal Digital Film Archive management.
    \item Marketplace participation.
    \item Community interaction and knowledge access.
    \item AI-powered photography assistance.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Film Lab Management Portal}

A web-based management portal will be developed for Film Lab owners and operators. The portal will support the digitalization of laboratory operations and allow service providers to manage their business processes more efficiently.

The main capabilities include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Film Lab profile management.
    \item Service package and pricing configuration.
    \item Customer order management.
    \item Processing workflow monitoring.
    \item Order status updates.
    \item Digital scan file upload and delivery.
    \item Customer information management.
    \item Revenue monitoring.
    \item Operational reports and business analytics.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Administration System}

A web-based administration system will be provided for platform administrators. The system will support platform governance, user management, content moderation, and overall operational monitoring.

Key features include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item User and role management.
    \item Film Lab approval and verification.
    \item Service category management.
    \item Marketplace monitoring.
    \item Transaction fee configuration.
    \item Payment monitoring.
    \item Review and community content moderation.
    \item Complaint and dispute management.
    \item Platform performance monitoring.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Marketplace Module}

The platform will include a marketplace for analog photography equipment trading. Community members will be able to buy, sell, or exchange cameras, lenses, film rolls, and photography accessories.

The marketplace module will provide:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Product listing management.
    \item Advanced product searching and filtering.
    \item Seller ratings and reviews.
    \item Buyer-seller communication.
    \item Transaction tracking.
    \item Marketplace moderation support.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Community and Knowledge-Sharing Platform}

A dedicated community module will support knowledge sharing and interaction among photographers, experts, and photography enthusiasts. Users will be able to access educational resources and participate in community activities.

The module will support:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Photography tutorials and technical articles.
    \item Film, camera, and lens reviews.
    \item Film Lab recommendations.
    \item Community discussions.
    \item Workshop registration.
    \item Photowalk event participation.
    \item Expert knowledge contribution.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Digital Film Archive Management System}

A Digital Film Archive will allow photographers to securely organize and preserve their scanned photographs. Digital images and related metadata will be stored in a structured manner for future access.

The archive will support:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Secure cloud storage for digital scans.
    \item Photo viewing and downloading.
    \item Album and collection organization.
    \item Search and filtering capabilities.
    \item Metadata management.
    \item Long-term digital preservation.
\end{itemize}

Stored metadata may include film stock, camera model, lens information, shooting date, processing method, Film Lab information, and scanning specifications.

\subsection{AI Recommendation Engine}

An AI-based recommendation engine will be developed to provide personalized suggestions to users. The system will analyze user preferences, service history, location, community feedback, and other relevant information.

The recommendation engine is expected to support:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Personalized Film Lab recommendations.
    \item Film type suggestions.
    \item Educational content recommendations.
    \item Community-based recommendations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{AI-Powered Photography Assistant}

A domain-specific AI Assistant will be integrated into the platform to support users with photography-related questions and recommendations. The assistant will combine Large Language Models with Retrieval-Augmented Generation to provide more contextual and relevant responses based on photography knowledge resources.

The AI Assistant will support:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Photography-related question answering.
    \item Film selection assistance.
    \item Technical photography guidance.
    \item Knowledge retrieval.
    \item Intelligent semantic search.
    \item Personalized user assistance.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Backend Services and Cloud Infrastructure}

The final deliverables will also include backend services, databases, APIs, cloud storage, AI services, and deployment infrastructure. These components will provide the technical foundation for communication between the mobile application, web portals, AI services, and storage systems.

The infrastructure will support scalability, security, maintainability, and future expansion of the platform.
