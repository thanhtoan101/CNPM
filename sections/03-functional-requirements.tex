\subsection*{Business Core Flows}

The functional requirements of the proposed platform are described through the
main business flows of the system. These flows show how photographers, Film
Labs, community members, and AI-supported services interact with each other on
the platform.

\subsubsection*{Core Flow 1. Intelligent Film Lab Discovery}
Photographers can search for Film Labs based on location, supported film
formats, available services, pricing, turnaround time, community ratings, or
special processing techniques. The platform collects information from multiple
labs and uses AI recommendation methods to suggest the most suitable options
based on user preferences and previous activities.

\subsubsection*{Core Flow 2. Service Booking and Film Collection}
After selecting a Film Lab, photographers create service orders by providing
film type, processing options, scanning quality, printing requirements, and
other requests. Customers can either bring film rolls directly or request a
pickup service through delivery partners. The Film Lab then checks the order
and arranges processing.

\subsubsection*{Core Flow 3. Film Processing Lifecycle Management}
Film Lab operators manage all stages of processing, including receiving film
rolls, chemical development, washing, drying, scanning, image preparation, and
final quality checking. Each step is recorded and synchronized with the
customer application so that users can follow the order progress in real time.

\subsubsection*{Core Flow 4. Digital Delivery and Film Archive Management}
After processing is completed, Film Labs upload digital scan files to secure
cloud storage. Customers receive notifications and can view, download,
organize, and store their scanned photographs in a personal Digital Film
Archive. The system also keeps metadata such as film stock, camera model,
lens, shooting date, processing method, and scanning specifications for later
reference.

\subsubsection*{Core Flow 5. Marketplace and Equipment Exchange}
Community members can post listings to buy, sell, or exchange analog cameras,
film rolls, lenses, and photography accessories. The platform supports listing
management, searching, seller ratings, transaction tracking, and communication
between buyers and sellers to create a clearer and more trustworthy trading
environment.

\subsubsection*{Core Flow 6. Knowledge Sharing and Community Engagement}
Photography experts and experienced community members can share tutorials,
technical articles, equipment reviews, lab recommendations, and photography
experiences. Users can also join discussions, register for workshops or
photowalk events, and contribute to the common knowledge base of the platform.

\subsection*{AI-powered Personalized Assistance}

Artificial Intelligence analyzes service history, user preferences, community
feedback, and knowledge resources to provide personalized Film Lab
recommendations, evaluate scan quality, answer technical questions, and
recommend suitable learning resources through an intelligent assistant.

The personalized assistance process includes the following main steps:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The photographer enters a question or selects a support topic.
    \item The system collects relevant information such as user preferences,
    previous service orders, saved Film Labs, and community feedback.
    \item The AI analyzes the request and returns suitable recommendations with
    short explanations.
    \item The photographer reviews the response, asks a follow-up question, or
    selects a recommended action.
    \item The system records user feedback to improve future recommendations.
\end{enumerate}

If there is not enough user history, the AI asks for basic preferences and
provides general suggestions. If the AI service is unavailable, users can still
use the normal search, filtering, and knowledge-base functions.

\subsection*{Main Functional Modules}

The platform is divided into the following main functional modules. Each
module supports one or more business core flows described above.

\subsubsection*{Module 1. Account and Profile Management}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Supports account registration, sign-in, sign-out, and account recovery.
    \item Manages personal information, photography interests, service
    preferences, and delivery addresses.
    \item Supports the Photographer, Film Lab Operator, Photography Expert, and
    System Administrator roles.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Module 2. Film Lab Discovery and Recommendation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Manages Film Lab profiles, services, prices, supported film formats,
    turnaround times, and locations.
    \item Allows photographers to search, filter, compare, rate, and review Film
    Labs.
    \item Recommends suitable Film Labs based on preferences, previous activity,
    and community feedback.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Module 3. Service Booking and Film Collection}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Allows photographers to select processing, scanning, printing, and
    other service options.
    \item Calculates estimated costs and supports direct delivery or pickup by a
    Delivery Partner.
    \item Creates service orders with order codes, confirmation information, and
    film handling instructions.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Module 4. Film Processing Lifecycle Management}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Allows Film Lab operators to receive, verify, and schedule film orders.
    \item Records development, washing, drying, scanning, image preparation, and
    quality-check stages.
    \item Synchronizes timestamps, notes, and order status so photographers can
    track progress in real time.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Module 5. Digital Delivery and Film Archive}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Uploads completed scans to secure cloud storage and links them to the
    correct order.
    \item Allows photographers to preview, download, organize, tag, and favorite
    photographs.
    \item Stores film, camera, lens, processing, and scanning metadata with access
    control.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Module 6. Marketplace and Equipment Exchange}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Allows users to create and manage listings for analog photography
    equipment and film.
    \item Supports search, buyer-seller communication, purchase or exchange
    requests, and transaction tracking.
    \item Supports ratings, cancellation, dispute reporting, and administrator
    review.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Module 7. Knowledge Sharing and Community}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Allows users to publish tutorials, articles, reviews, and photography
    experiences.
    \item Supports discussions, comments, questions, workshops, photowalks, and
    event registration.
    \item Supports content reporting and moderation to maintain community
    quality.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Module 8. AI-powered Personalized Assistance}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Recommends Film Labs and learning resources based on user needs.
    \item Identifies common scan-quality problems such as dust, color cast, low
    contrast, or incorrect exposure.
    \item Answers technical questions using the platform knowledge base and
    collects feedback for later recommendations.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Module 9. Notification and Administration}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Notifies users about orders, pickup, processing progress, digital
    delivery, marketplace transactions, and events.
    \item Allows administrators to manage users, Film Labs, services, reported
    content, and disputes.
    \item Records important system activities and provides basic management
    reports.
\end{itemize}
