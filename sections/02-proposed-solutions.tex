\section*{3.2.b Proposed Solutions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{3.2.b Proposed Solutions}

The proposed platform serves as an integrated ecosystem that connects film photography enthusiasts, Film Labs, photography experts, delivery partners, and administrators within a unified digital environment. The objective is to digitalize the entire film photography workflow while enhancing community engagement and leveraging Artificial Intelligence (AI) to provide intelligent recommendations and personalized services.

\subsection*{Overall Solution}

The platform enables photographers to search, compare, and select Film Labs based on service quality, pricing, turnaround time, location, and community reviews. Users can place service orders, request pickup and delivery services, track processing progress in real time, receive digital scans, and organize their scanned photographs within a personal Digital Film Archive.

Film Labs are provided with a dedicated management portal that supports order management, workflow scheduling, customer communication, digital file delivery, business analytics, and revenue monitoring.

In addition to service management, the platform includes a Marketplace module where users can buy and sell cameras, films, lenses, and photography accessories. A Knowledge Sharing module allows experts to publish tutorials, technical articles, reviews, and educational content, thereby fostering a sustainable learning environment for the analog photography community.

Artificial Intelligence is integrated throughout the platform to deliver personalized Film Lab recommendations, intelligent search capabilities, film selection assistance, image quality analysis, and a domain-specific AI Assistant capable of answering photography-related questions.

\subsection*{Platform Ecosystem}

The ecosystem consists of the following interconnected participants:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Film Photographers and Customers
    \item Film Processing Laboratories (Film Labs)
    \item Photography Experts
    \item Delivery Partners
    \item Marketplace Participants
    \item AI Services and Recommendation Engines
    \item System Administrators
\end{itemize}

These participants collaborate within a centralized platform that supports service booking, order tracking, digital asset management, community interaction, and intelligent assistance.

\subsection*{System Roles}

\textbf{Photographer / Customer}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Register and manage personal accounts.
    \item Search and compare Film Labs.
    \item Book film developing, scanning, and printing services.
    \item Request pickup and delivery services.
    \item Track order progress in real time.
    \item Download and manage digital scans.
    \item Maintain a personal Digital Film Archive.
    \item Rate and review Film Labs.
    \item Buy and sell photography equipment.
    \item Interact with the AI Assistant.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Film Lab Owner}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Manage laboratory profiles.
    \item Configure service packages and pricing.
    \item Receive customer orders.
    \item Update processing status.
    \item Upload digital scan files.
    \item Monitor revenue and business performance.
    \item Generate reports.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Photography Expert}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Publish educational articles and tutorials.
    \item Share photography techniques and experiences.
    \item Review films, cameras, lenses, and laboratories.
    \item Organize workshops and community events.
    \item Contribute knowledge to the AI knowledge base.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Delivery Partner}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Receive pickup requests.
    \item Collect film rolls from customers.
    \item Deliver films to Film Labs.
    \item Return processed films to customers.
    \item Update delivery status.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{System Administrator}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Manage users and permissions.
    \item Approve Film Labs.
    \item Manage marketplace activities.
    \item Monitor transactions and payments.
    \item Moderate community content.
    \item Resolve complaints and disputes.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{AI Assistant}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Recommend suitable Film Labs.
    \item Suggest appropriate film types.
    \item Answer photography-related questions.
    \item Support intelligent search and personalized recommendations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Research-Based Learning (RBL)}

The project incorporates Research-Based Learning by investigating advanced technologies that can enhance user experience and platform intelligence. The main research topics include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Recommendation Systems
    \item Computer Vision for Image Quality Assessment
    \item Semantic Search
    \item Knowledge Graphs
    \item Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
    \item Large Language Models (LLMs)
    \item Community-based Recommendation
    \item Digital Asset Management
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Research Approach}

The project investigates recommendation algorithms for service matching, Computer Vision models for scan quality assessment, and semantic retrieval techniques for photography knowledge management. It also explores the integration of Large Language Models with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to develop a domain-specific AI Assistant capable of providing accurate and contextual photography guidance.

Comparative evaluations will be conducted to identify suitable AI models that achieve high recommendation accuracy, reliable image analysis, and effective conversational support. Through this research-driven approach, the platform aims to establish a scalable and sustainable digital ecosystem for the analog photography community.
